What is measured

A structured snapshot of indoor conditions.

Readings reflect conditions at the time and place of the visit. They are screening data—not a diagnosis, clearance test or declaration that a property is safe or unsafe.

Total VOCsBroad volatile-organic-compound screening
FormaldehydeScreening measurement by room

Material moistureTargeted surface/building-material checks

The visit

A repeatable path through the property.

  1. Opening interview and property context
  2. Exterior and mechanical-system orientation
  3. Room-by-room readings and observation notes
  4. Targeted moisture checks where indicated
  5. Floor-plan annotation and report assembly

Optional laboratory kits can identify specific chemicals. Laboratory fees, sampling strategy and turnaround are quoted separately.
