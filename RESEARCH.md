# AirWave Audit reference-site research

Research date: 2026-11-24. Inspiration only; no copy, imagery, logos, code or exact layouts were reused.

| Site | What it does best | What it does poorly / lesson |
|---|---|---|
| [Indoor Doctor](https://www.indoordoctor.com/) | Strong local credibility, service taxonomy, media proof and concrete statistics. | Dense navigation and health-forward language can feel alarmist; use calmer scope-first copy. |
| [Indoor Science](https://indoorscience.com/what-we-do/indoor-air-quality/air-quality/) | Explains why broad inspection precedes targeted sampling and distinguishes testing categories. | Long technical copy delays booking; summarize scope early. |
| [FieldWise EMF](https://fieldwiseemf.com/) | Plain-language field-type explanation, transparent pricing and direct discovery-call path. | Precautionary health framing is outside AirWave's measurement-only position. |
| [Create Healthy Homes](https://createhealthyhomes.com/) | Deep methodology, expertise and detailed deliverables. | Sprawling information architecture and dated visual hierarchy. |
| [Healthy Home Biology](https://healthyhomebiology.com/) | Warm residential imagery, regional messaging and clear service cards. | Too many categories compete for attention; preserve two-service focus. |
| [Air Quality Testers](https://airqualitytesters.com/) | Independent-testing message and clear laboratory distinction. | The deliverable is not immediately tangible; lead with maps and report previews. |
| [Oasis Indoor Environmental](https://www.oasisie.com/) | Longevity, geographic credibility and obvious audience split. | Conventional contractor styling reduces premium feel. |
| [Test My Air](https://testmyair.com/) | Excellent step flow, family story and productized report-oriented offer. | Credential and health language is heavy; use only substantiated neutral claims. |
| [Home Inspection Carolina](https://www.homeinspectioncarolina.com/) | Best-in-class sample-report presentation, immediate scheduling and agent resources. | Broad inspection catalog would distract from AirWave's specialist focus. |
| [Pillar To Post](https://langteam.pillartopost.com/book-home-inspection/) | Tiered packages, benefits grid and booking-oriented productization. | Feature lists become overwhelming; show only decision-relevant differences. |

## Best-of components adapted

1. One-line outcome hero for immediate comprehension.
2. Two-service decision cards for specialist clarity.
3. Deliverable-first storytelling with original sample maps.
4. Transparent package pricing for time-sensitive buyers.
5. Four-step process to make a technical service predictable.
6. Methodology and limitations presented together.
7. Local trust strip focused on insured operation and property experience.
8. Simple referral workflow for agents and attorneys.
9. Print-ready one-page professional referral sheet.
10. Accordion FAQs near conversion points.
11. A single calm teal accent instead of hazard colors.
12. Persistent, low-friction booking CTA.
