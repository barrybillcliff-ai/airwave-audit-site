Methodology summary

Readable without overreaching.

Every report separates measured observations from context and interpretation.

Report element What is included What is not claimed
Property & scope Address, date, requested service, accessible areas Areas not observed
Method Instrument category, units, route, measurement points Laboratory equivalence unless separately sampled
Results Tables, floor/property maps, source-context notes Health diagnosis or safe/unsafe conclusion
Reference context Applicable published reference values where relevant Legal or regulatory opinion
