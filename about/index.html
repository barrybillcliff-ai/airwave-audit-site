About

Local building experience. Calm, careful documentation.

AirWave Audit was created to give families and real-estate professionals a clearer record of indoor conditions and property-level signal patterns.

Already trusted inside area homes.

Barry also operates Merrimack Valley Roofing. That work has put him in hundreds of local homes and built a practical understanding of assemblies, moisture pathways, ventilation details and the importance of explaining property conditions without drama.

AirWave Audit applies the same local accountability to a narrower mission: collect a consistent set of readings, document where they were taken and give the client a usable visual record.

Working principles

Measured, bounded and transparent.

State the limits

Every report explains what the survey can and cannot establish.

Separate observation from inference

Readings, site context and likely-source notes are labeled distinctly.

Protect client data

Property photos and reports are shared only with authorized recipients.

Refer specialist scopes

Radon, mold, lead and asbestos work goes to appropriately qualified providers.
