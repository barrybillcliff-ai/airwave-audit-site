Is this a health or safety assessment?

No. AirWave Audit provides measurement, screening and documentation. It does not diagnose health conditions or label a property safe or unsafe.

Do you test for mold, radon, lead or asbestos?

Those are specialist scopes. We may document relevant observations or moisture readings and refer clients to properly qualified providers for those services.

How are RF readings presented?

Readings are mapped by location with the instrument, units and survey method identified. FCC limits may be included for reference; the service is not a health assessment.
