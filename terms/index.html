Service terms & limitations

Effective: November 25, 2026

Scope

The agreed service is a limited, non-invasive screening and documentation survey of accessible areas under conditions present at the time. The written proposal or confirmation controls the scope.

No health, legal or engineering opinion

Services do not diagnose health conditions, establish causation, provide legal advice or constitute an engineering evaluation. Reports do not declare a property safe or unsafe. RF/EMF readings may be compared with FCC limits for reference only.

Screening and laboratory work

Handheld readings are screening measurements and do not identify every chemical or replace compound-specific analysis. Any laboratory sampling is separately scoped and subject to laboratory methods, detection limits and terms.

Excluded specialist services

Radon, mold, lead and asbestos services are excluded unless expressly stated and performed through an appropriately qualified provider.

Access and conditions

The client must arrange lawful access and disclose known hazards. Concealed, inaccessible or unsafe areas are excluded. Conditions may vary before or after the visit.

Scheduling, cancellation and payment

Any deposit, cancellation, rescheduling, travel or payment terms that apply will be stated in the written quote before you approve an appointment. A website request alone does not create a charge.

Reports and reliance

The report is prepared for the named client and intended recipients identified in writing. Third-party reliance requires written agreement. Example maps on this website contain illustrative data only.
