Frequently asked questions

What the survey does—and what it does not.

Straight answers about scope, measurement, reporting and limitations.

Is an indoor air survey a health assessment?

No. It is a detailed screening and mapping survey. AirWave Audit does not diagnose a condition, determine the cause of symptoms or declare a property safe or unsafe. Health questions belong with an appropriate medical professional.

What does “screening” mean?

Handheld instruments provide useful readings for comparison across locations and at a point in time. They do not identify every compound or replace compound-specific laboratory analysis.

Can you identify a specific chemical?

Optional laboratory kits may be available when a specific analytical question is defined. The sampling plan, laboratory, fee and limitations are agreed separately.

Do you test for mold, radon, lead or asbestos?

Not within the standard service. We may record moisture or visual context and refer those specialist scopes to appropriately qualified providers.

Is RF/EMF mapping a health assessment?

No. It is measurement and documentation. Readings may be compared with FCC limits for reference only; the report does not offer medical conclusions or an opinion that a location is safe or unsafe.

What affects a reading?

Occupancy, ventilation, weather, open windows, appliances, wireless traffic, nearby devices and other temporary conditions can affect measurements. Conditions and material limitations are stated in the report.

How quickly is the report delivered?

The standard target is two to three business days after the visit. Rush timing may be available by prior agreement.

Can my agent or attorney receive the report?

Yes, with your authorization. Reports and property photos are not shared with third parties without permission except as required by law.

Do you sell remediation or shielding?

No. The core service is independent measurement and documentation. Likely source categories and reasonable next investigative steps may be described.

What areas do you serve?

Southern New Hampshire, the Merrimack Valley and the North Shore of Massachusetts. Travel outside the core area may be available by quote.
